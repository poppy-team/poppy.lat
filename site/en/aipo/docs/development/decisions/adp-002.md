# ADP-002 — Construction Hooks and Runtime Contracts

- **Status**: Approved & Implemented
- **Deciders**: Poppy Team
- **Phase**: Wave 1 (P00-G13 / P00-G14)

---

## Context

The language needed a unified, low-cognitive-overhead mechanism to validate struct states without requiring developers to write verbose boilerplate constructors or artificial getters and setters.

---

## Decisions Taken

1. **`init()` Hook**:
   - Executes immediately after struct field assignment during instantiation.
   - Used for structural validation, normalization, and rejecting invalid initial states with `fail`.

2. **`invariant()` Invariants**:
   - Declared directly in the `struct` body.
   - Evaluated upon creation and on all stable field mutations.
   - Predicate failures immediately reject the mutation.

3. **Transactional Rollback inside `attempt`**:
   - When mutations occur inside an `attempt` block, the VM maintains an in-memory rollback journal.
   - Any failure automatically restores pre-block state, guaranteeing atomic consistency.
